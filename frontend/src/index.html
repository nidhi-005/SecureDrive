Welcome to SecureDrive

Secure, passwordless account access.

Protect your files

Create a separate password to protect your Master Key. This password is not your passkey.

Unlock your files

Your passkey has been verified. Enter your separate file encryption password to unlock your Master Key.

Authentication uses a local-device WebAuthn passkey. File encryption uses AES-256-GCM with a separate PBKDF2-derived key. Your file-encryption password is never sent to the server. After a page reload, unlock your files again with that password.
