Welcome to SecureDrive

Secure, passwordless account access.

On this device, use its fingerprint, face recognition or PIN. On a different device, choose the phone / QR option and follow the browser's passkey prompt. QR availability depends on your browser, operating system and registered phone passkey.

Protect your files

Create a separate password to protect your Master Key. This password is not your passkey.

Unlock your files

Your passkey has been verified. Enter your separate file encryption password to unlock your Master Key.

Register another device

Add a passkey to this device, or use your phone to create a cross-device passkey through the browser's QR flow.

Authentication uses WebAuthn passkeys, including compatible synced and cross-device authenticators. File encryption uses AES-256-GCM with a separate PBKDF2-derived key. Your file-encryption password is never sent to the server. After a page reload, unlock your files again with that password.
